# Learning resources (videos, PDFs, tutorials)

⭐ = start here. Each daily lesson links to the exact chapter or video you need.

## Causal inference foundations
- ⭐ Brady Neal, *Introduction to Causal Inference*: free video lectures + PDF textbook. https://www.bradyneal.com/causal-inference-course
- ⭐ Cunningham, *Causal Inference: The Mixtape* (free online book). https://mixtape.scunning.com/
- Huntington-Klein, *The Effect* (free online book + companion YouTube videos). https://theeffectbook.net/
- Facure, *Causal Inference for the Brave and True* (free, Python). https://matheusfacure.github.io/python-causality-handbook/
- Hernán & Robins, *What If* (free PDF), and Hernán's free edX course *Causal Diagrams*

## A/B testing and online experimentation
- ⭐ Kohavi, Tang & Xu, *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments* (book; the core reference for this role)
- ⭐ Udacity, *A/B Testing* (by Google): free video course
- Ron Kohavi's talks (YouTube) and paper list (experimentguide.com)
- Microsoft ExP research papers (CUPED, SRM, metric sensitivity)
