Rent Abatement Settlement Agreement

Dated: June 18, 2026

Definitions of Terms / Parties

"Tenant"   Maxim L. Pak & Sidney R. Palomino

"Rental Property Owner"   Chetan Kulkarni & Shraddha Phadnis

"Property Manager / Broker"   Johnson & Associates Real Estate Services (Jody Johnson, DRE #01776542)

"Premises / Property"   10950 Ivy Hill Drive #4, San Diego, CA 92131

"Lease"   Residential Lease dated July 17, 2024, between Tenant, Rental Property Owner, and Property Manager for the Premises

"Incident"   Water heater failure reported by Tenant on April 13, 2026, resulting in an approved insurance claim for Owner and a denied renter's insurance claim for Tenant. The Property was deemed habitable by both insurance companies. This is considered a no-fault incident consistent with normal wear and tear.

"Phase 1"   Water mitigation for kitchen and garage, including dry-out and removal of wet drywall, kitchen laminate, and one kitchen cabinet. Tenant retained partial kitchen access. (Completed April 16–20, 2026)

"Phase 2"   Rebuild work including drywall restoration, cabinetry replacement, and related interior repairs, excluding replacement flooring and ceiling paint. (Completed June 12, 2026)

"Phase 3"   Replacement flooring and ceiling paint. (Scheduled to begin August 1, 2026, following lease expiration and Tenant move-out)

"Abatement Period"   April 13, 2026 through June 12, 2026 (61 days)

"Loss of Use"   Interference with Tenant's reasonable enjoyment and use of the Premises during the Abatement Period, resulting from the Incident and associated repair work

Recitals

Tenant and Rental Property Owner agree that the Incident — a water heater failure occurring on or about April 13, 2026 — caused a temporary disruption to the Premises during the Abatement Period. A temporary water heater was installed on April 15, 2026 to restore hot water service to Tenant. Phase 1 water mitigation was completed on April 20, 2026. Phase 2 rebuild work was completed on June 12, 2026. Asbestos testing conducted on April 14, 2026 confirmed no asbestos was present in the affected area.

Although the Property was deemed habitable by both Tenant's and Owner's insurance companies throughout the Abatement Period, Owner, in good faith, is offering and Tenant is accepting the rent abatement credit set forth herein in acknowledgment of the Loss of Use Tenant experienced during the Abatement Period.

Rent Abatement Credit

Owner and Tenant agree that a 50% rent abatement totaling $3,600.00 for the full Abatement Period (April 13, 2026 – June 12, 2026) represents a fair and reasonable resolution of all claims arising from the Incident.

Monthly Rent:$3,600.00
Agreed Abatement Rate:50%
Total Settlement Credit:$3,600.00
Applied To:July 2026 Rent
July 2026 Rent Due from Tenant:$0.00

The $3,600.00 credit shall be applied in full toward Tenant's July 2026 rent obligation. No rent payment shall be due from Tenant for the month of July 2026.

Other Terms

  • Tenant agrees that no further rent abatement, credit, or compensation of any kind is owed beyond the credit expressly described in this Agreement.
  • Tenant agrees to vacate the Premises and return possession to Owner in good and clean condition on or before July 31, 2026, in accordance with the Lease terms.
  • Owner agrees to complete Phase 3 work (replacement flooring and ceiling paint) after July 31, 2026, following Tenant's move-out, so that Tenant's remaining tenancy is not further disrupted.
  • Both parties agree to conduct the conclusion of the tenancy in a professional and cooperative manner through July 31, 2026.
  • This settlement does not constitute an admission of liability or wrongdoing by any party.

Opportunity to Consult Counsel

Each party acknowledges having had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel prior to signing this Agreement and enters into it voluntarily, with full understanding of its terms and legal effect.

Release of Claims

In consideration of the rent abatement credit described in this Agreement, Tenant (Maxim L. Pak and Sidney R. Palomino) hereby releases and discharges Rental Property Owner (Chetan Kulkarni and Shraddha Phadnis), Property Manager / Broker (Johnson & Associates Real Estate Services and Jody Johnson), and each of their respective agents, employees, contractors, and insurers (collectively, the "Released Parties"), from any and all claims, demands, damages, losses, liabilities, and causes of action arising out of or relating to the Incident and the repair work performed during the Abatement Period, including but not limited to claims for rent abatement, loss of use, inconvenience, emotional distress, personal property damage, or breach of the implied warranty of habitability, as those claims relate specifically to the Incident.

In consideration of Tenant's agreement to settle and vacate, Rental Property Owner releases Tenant from any claims directly related to the Incident as defined herein.

California Civil Code §1542 Waiver: The parties expressly acknowledge and waive the provisions of California Civil Code §1542, which provides: "A general release does not extend to claims that the creditor or releasing party does not know or suspect to exist in his or her favor at the time of executing the release and that, if known by him or her, would have materially affected his or her settlement with the debtor or releasing party." The parties intend this release to cover all claims arising from the Incident, whether currently known or unknown to either party.

Agreement Not to Pursue Further Claims Related to the Incident

Both Tenant and Owner acknowledge that this Agreement represents a full and final resolution of all claims between the parties arising from the Incident and the repair work performed during the Abatement Period. Neither party shall initiate any legal proceeding, arbitration, or administrative action against the other party specifically related to the Incident as defined in this Agreement. This section does not prevent either party from pursuing claims arising from separate, independent events occurring after the execution of this Agreement.

Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between Tenant and Owner concerning the Incident and supersedes all prior oral or written discussions, negotiations, and agreements regarding the same. No modification of this Agreement shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by all parties. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. By signing below, all parties agree to the terms set forth in this Agreement.
